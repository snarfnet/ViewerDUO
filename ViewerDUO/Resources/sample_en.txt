How to use Viewer DUO

Chapter 1 Welcome

Viewer DUO brings the feel of turning real paper to the screen. Each page is simulated as a physical sheet: it bends with your finger, casts a shadow on the page beneath, and settles at a natural speed when you let go.

This book is a sample placed in your library the first time the app starts. Delete it whenever you like. Press and hold a book to rename or delete it.

Start by resting a finger on the right edge of the page and pulling slowly to the left. The paper lifts and the back shows through. Let go early and it falls back; pass the middle and it turns to the next page.

Chapter 2 Turning pages

There are three ways to turn.

The first is dragging. Pull from an edge and the corner of the sheet follows your finger. Pinching a corner gives a big diagonal curl; pulling from the middle of the edge folds the page straight over.

The second is tapping. Tap near an edge and the corner lifts and turns on its own. Tap the opposite edge to go back.

The third is the slider at the bottom, useful for long books. The table of contents and your bookmarks also jump straight to a page.

Tap the centre of the screen to hide the control panels when you want to read without distraction. Tap again to bring them back.

Chapter 3 Spread and single page

On a wide screen the book opens as a two-page spread: an iPad on its side, an iPhone in landscape, or an iPhone Duo unfolded. On iPhone Duo the hinge becomes the spine of the book.

In a spread, folding the right page to the left reveals its back as the new left page, and a fresh right page appears beneath it, exactly like a printed book.

The layout button in the bottom panel switches between automatic, single page and spread. For manga or vertical Japanese text, turn on right-to-left so pages run from right to left. This can be set per book.

Chapter 4 Paper and desk

Choose from six papers and six desks. White, cream, parchment, sepia, moss and night; walnut, marble, indigo linen, leather, obsidian and ivory. Mix them freely.

For reading at night, try night paper on the obsidian desk with the warm filter raised a little. In a bright room, cream paper on marble is easy on the eyes.

Text books can be set in serif, sans, rounded, Mincho or Gothic, at any size, line spacing and margin. Vertical Japanese writing is supported. Change a setting and the book is re-set on the spot, keeping your place.

Chapter 5 Formats

Viewer DUO opens PDF, EPUB, TXT, ZIP/CBZ and RAR/CBR. Use Add books, or choose Viewer DUO from another app's share sheet.

PDFs keep their original layout, and their outline becomes the table of contents. EPUB and TXT are re-set in your chosen paper and typeface. Images inside ZIP and RAR archives become pages in file-name order.

Full-text search works for PDF, EPUB and TXT. Use the magnifier in the top-right corner.

Chapter 6 Bookmarks and memory

The bookmark button in the top-right corner marks the page you are on; press it again to remove the mark. Bookmarks sit in the tab beside the table of contents, ready whenever you need them.

Your position is remembered every time you close a book, and the library shows a Continue reading card at the top.

Every book stays on this device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Enjoy your reading.
